# DSP-Lab Parallel Test Expert Review Form

## Purpose

This form is intended for 2–3 instructors with experience teaching undergraduate Digital Signal Processing (DSP). The goal is to review the researcher-developed parallel concept tests used in DSP-Lab:

- Form A: 12-item pre-test
- Form B: 12-item post-test

The review should focus on content relevance, wording clarity, and equivalence between the paired Form A/Form B items. The instrument should not be described as validated until this review and a student pilot have been completed.

## Rating scale

For each criterion, use a 4-point scale:

1 = not acceptable / major revision required
2 = partly acceptable / substantial revision required
3 = acceptable / minor revision may be useful
4 = highly acceptable / no revision required

### Criteria

- **Relevance**: Does the item appropriately assess the intended DSP learning objective?
- **Clarity**: Is the wording understandable and unambiguous for undergraduate students?
- **Parallel-form equivalence**: Do the Form A and Form B items assess the same construct at approximately the same cognitive level and difficulty?

## Suggested content-validity summary

For research reporting, ratings of 3 or 4 can be treated as content-valid endorsements.

- **I-CVI** for an item/criterion = number of experts rating 3 or 4 / total number of experts.
- **S-CVI/Ave** = average of I-CVI values across the reviewed items.
- With only three experts, disagreement should be discussed qualitatively rather than relying on a numerical threshold alone.

A revision log should be retained showing the original wording, expert comment, decision, and final wording.
