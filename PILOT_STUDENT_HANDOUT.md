# DSP-Lab Concept-Test Pilot — Student Instructions

## Why you are being asked to participate

We are checking the clarity and approximate comparability of two short Digital Signal Processing concept tests before they are used in a teaching study.

This session is **not an examination of the DSP-Lab application** and is not intended to measure the effectiveness of a teaching intervention.

## What you will do

You will complete two short DSP concept-test forms:

- Form A
- Form B

Each form contains 12 multiple-choice questions and normally takes about **10–15 minutes**.

Some students will complete A first and B second; others will complete B first and A second. This is intentional.

## During the session

Please:

- work independently;
- use the same calculator policy stated by the instructor for both forms;
- do not discuss answers with other participants;
- do not search for answers online;
- do not use DSP-Lab between the two forms;
- do not ask for answer hints during the test.

A short neutral break will be provided between the two forms.

## After both forms

You may be asked to identify any question that was unclear, awkwardly worded, or seemed to have more than one reasonable answer.

Useful comments include:

- “The wording of Item A4 was unclear.”
- “Two choices in B7 seemed possible.”
- “I did not understand the term used in A10.”

You do **not** need to explain why you got an item wrong.

## Participant code

Use only the study code provided to you, such as `P01` or `P02`.

Do not write your name, student number, email address, or other direct identifier on the pilot response sheet unless required by the institution's approved procedure outside the research dataset.

## Important note

No item-by-item answer feedback will be given until both forms have been completed. This helps the research team compare the two forms without a teaching effect occurring between them.

Thank you for helping improve the assessment materials.
