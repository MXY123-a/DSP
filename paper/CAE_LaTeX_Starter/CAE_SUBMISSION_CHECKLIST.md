# CAE Submission Checklist

This checklist is intentionally short. It reflects the current *Computer Applications in Engineering Education* author guidance and is meant for the final submission stage only.

## Main manuscript

- Keep the anonymous main text free of author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and identifying ethics/institution details until the blind-review version is prepared.
- Abstract: no more than 250 words.
- Keywords: use exactly **five** keywords in the final submission version.
- Keep the current simple paper structure: Introduction; Related Work and Design Rationale; DSP-Lab Design and Implementation; Teaching Evaluation; Results and Discussion; Conclusion.
- Do not expand the study with additional groups, outcomes, or statistical models unless a genuine study issue requires it.

## Title page

- Use a short informative article title.
- Include a running title shorter than 40 characters.
- Include full author names, affiliations, corresponding-author details, acknowledgments, and funding information as applicable.

## Tables and figures

- Keep publication-quality figure source files available separately.
- At final submission, prepare figures as separate files and provide figure legends in the manuscript as required by the submission system.
- Ensure tables have clear titles and can be supplied in the manuscript in the order requested by the journal.

## References

- Use numbered in-text citations in square brackets in the final formatted submission.
- Verify every reference against the publisher/DOI record before submission.
- Do not add unverified references simply to enlarge the literature review.

## Final study reporting

- Report the actual participant numbers and group allocation method.
- Report Form A/Form B expert review and pilot testing briefly; detailed item diagnostics can remain supplementary.
- Main effectiveness result: baseline-adjusted post-test group difference from `PostScore ~ Group + PreScore`.
- Questionnaire findings remain secondary and descriptive.
- State the main limitations directly, especially single-institution/short-duration and intact-class confounding if applicable.
- Add the final ethics/consent statement and Data Availability Statement.

## Final packaging

Prepare separate submission files for:

1. title page;
2. anonymous main manuscript;
3. figures;
4. supplementary material, if retained.

The current LaTeX PDF is a working manuscript layout. Repackage tables/figures only at the final submission stage so the writing workflow stays simple during data collection and analysis.
