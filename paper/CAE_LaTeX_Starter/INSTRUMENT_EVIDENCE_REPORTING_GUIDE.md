# Instrument Evidence Reporting Guide

This file defines how expert-review and pilot evidence should be compressed into the CAE manuscript after the real data are available. The goal is to strengthen measurement credibility without letting instrument development dominate the main paper.

## Main-text target

Keep the expert-review + pilot evidence to approximately one compact paragraph in Methods and, if useful, one short sentence in Results or the Supplementary Material reference. Detailed item diagnostics belong in `supplementary_material.tex`.

### Recommended Methods paragraph after data collection

Replace bracketed fields only with observed values:

> The two researcher-developed 12-item concept forms underwent expert content review by **[N]** instructors with experience teaching undergraduate DSP. Reviewers independently rated item relevance, clarity, distractor quality, cognitive-level alignment, and A/B pair equivalence. The relevance review yielded an S-CVI/Ave of **[value]**; **[count]** item(s)/pair(s) required substantive revision before pilot testing. The revised forms were then administered to **[pilot N]** students using a counterbalanced AB/BA sequence with no DSP instruction or answer feedback between administrations. Mean scores were **[M_A (SD_A)]** for Form A and **[M_B (SD_B)]** for Form B; the paired B-minus-A difference was **[difference]** points (95% CI **[lower, upper]**, Cohen's dz = **[value]**). Item difficulty, corrected item-total correlations, floor/ceiling behavior, and sequence sensitivity were inspected jointly before the forms were frozen. Detailed evidence is provided in Supplementary Tables S1-S5.

Do **not** insert the sentence above before the real expert review and pilot have occurred.

## What should stay out of the main paper

Avoid placing all 24 item statistics, every expert comment, every revision note, or every AB/BA subgroup statistic in the main manuscript. Those details are useful for transparency but consume space without strengthening the central learning-effect narrative. Put them in the supplementary template instead.

## Minimum values to retain for the final manuscript package

Record the following before instrument freeze:

- final expert-review N;
- S-CVI/Ave for relevance;
- item/pair IDs below the prespecified relevance criterion;
- number and nature of substantive revisions;
- final pilot N and AB/BA counts;
- Form A and B mean, SD, and score range;
- paired B-A difference with 95% CI;
- Cohen's dz for the paired difference;
- A/B total-score correlation;
- Form A and B internal-consistency estimate, interpreted cautiously;
- item difficulty and corrected item-total correlation;
- item-pair difficulty differences;
- floor/ceiling proportions;
- evidence of sequence/order sensitivity;
- final freeze date and version/commit.

## Interpretation guardrails

The paper should continue to use the phrase **researcher-developed parallel concept tests**. Expert CVI supports content-relevance evidence; it does not prove construct validity. A non-significant paired A/B test is not evidence of equivalence by itself. Internal consistency for a short, multidomain 12-item concept test may be moderate and should not be overinterpreted. If an item is retained despite a weak diagnostic, the reason should be substantive and documented.

## Decision logic after pilot

Use converging evidence rather than a mechanical single cutoff. A revision is most defensible when at least one of the following occurs together with a plausible content problem: repeated expert concern, repeated student clarity complaint, extreme difficulty, weak discrimination, unusually large paired-item difficulty difference, or clear sequence sensitivity. Avoid repeatedly tuning items to maximize alpha or to force identical A/B means.

## Link to the main study

Once the forms are frozen, do not change item wording, answer keys, scoring, or administration rules after main-study outcomes begin to accumulate unless a dated protocol amendment is unavoidable. Any such change must be reported transparently.

## Publication priority

The main CAE paper remains an intervention-effect paper. Instrument evidence should be strong enough to make the learning outcome credible, but the manuscript should not become a psychometric-validation paper. The preferred balance is:

**brief Methods summary + supplementary item evidence + one prespecified primary ANCOVA result.**
