\section*{Purpose}
This supplementary document is a pre-results reporting template. It should be completed only with observed expert-review and pilot data. The two 12-item forms should continue to be described as \emph{researcher-developed parallel concept tests}; the evidence reported here supports content review and preliminary form comparability but does not, by itself, establish broad construct validity.

\section*{S2. Counterbalanced pilot}
The pilot uses an AB/BA counterbalanced administration. Approximately half of participants complete Form A followed by Form B and the remainder complete Form B followed by Form A. No DSP instruction, answer feedback, or DSP-Lab practice should occur between forms.

\section*{S3. Freeze decision}
Before the main study begins, document the final decision for each item pair and record the date on which the forms were frozen. Revisions should be driven by converging evidence from expert review, item diagnostics, repeated clarity comments, or clear form imbalance rather than by a single arbitrary cutoff.

\paragraph{Interpretation rule.}
A non-significant test of the mean A/B difference must not be presented as proof that the forms are equivalent. The reporting decision should consider the estimated mean difference and confidence interval, item-pair diagnostics, score distributions, order sensitivity, expert judgments, and substantive DSP content alignment together.
