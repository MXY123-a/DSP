## Verification rules for future additions

1. Do not add a reference only because an AI system, blog, or secondary citation mentions it.
2. Verify title, authors, journal, year, volume/issue/pages or article number, and DOI against the publisher page or Crossref-quality metadata.
3. For the final manuscript, prioritize directly relevant DSP/engineering-education studies and systematic reviews/meta-analyses; avoid inflating the bibliography with loosely related educational-technology papers.
4. Distinguish what a cited paper actually demonstrated. A platform-description or satisfaction study should not be cited as evidence of causal learning improvement.
5. If a DOI cannot be confirmed, retain the reference only when a trustworthy institutional or journal record clearly verifies the publication; note the absence of a DOI.
6. Before submission, run a final DOI and bibliographic-field audit of every entry in `references.bib`.

## Current publication-oriented literature strategy

The manuscript should keep a compact evidence chain:

- **Direct DSP predecessors:** J-DSP, CAE instructional software, CAE DSP toolkit, AJDSP, Jupyter–Python DSP course.
- **Mobile-learning evidence:** Sung et al. (2016), Garzón et al. (2025), plus one recent CAE engineering-course example.
- **Engineering interactive/virtual-lab evidence:** Singh et al. (2021), Nolen & Koretsky (2018), and the most relevant reviews/meta-analysis.
- **Pedagogical rationale:** Freeman et al. (2014) for active learning.

This is intentionally narrower than a comprehensive review because the paper is designed as an empirical CAE study, not a systematic review article.
