# DSP-Lab Concept Tests — Expert Review Invitation and Instructions

## Purpose

Thank you for reviewing the two short DSP concept tests developed for the DSP-Lab teaching study.

- Form A: 12-item pre-test
- Form B: 12-item post-test
- Target learners: undergraduate students taking Digital Signal Processing
- Review purpose: check content relevance, clarity, distractor quality, and approximate A/B comparability before classroom use

This is a content review for a teaching-research study. It is not intended to establish a full psychometric validation study.

## Who should review

The preferred panel is **2–3 university instructors** with experience teaching undergraduate DSP or closely related signal-processing courses.

Please record only the professional information needed for the manuscript, such as teaching role, years of DSP teaching experience, and main teaching/research area. Names are not required in the research summary unless permission and institutional policy support identification.

## Materials to use

Please review:

1. `docs/EXPERT_REVIEW_PACKET.md`
2. the 12 matched Form A/Form B item pairs in that packet

For each pair, rate:

- Relevance
- Clarity
- Distractor quality
- A/B equivalence

Use the 4-point scale already printed in the review packet.

## What we most need from you

Please focus on practical teaching quality rather than small stylistic preferences.

Flag an item when you believe that:

- the keyed answer is incorrect or debatable;
- the wording could reasonably confuse a typical undergraduate DSP student;
- more than one option could be defended;
- the item does not match the stated learning objective;
- the paired Form A/B items differ noticeably in cognitive demand or likely difficulty;
- terminology differs from normal undergraduate DSP usage.

Minor wording preferences may be noted, but they do not require revision unless they affect interpretation.

## Approximate review time

A complete review should normally take about **20–30 minutes**.

## Final recommendation

At the end of the packet, please select one:

- Accept for pilot testing without revision
- Accept for pilot testing after minor revision
- Major revision required before pilot testing

Please identify only the items that genuinely need attention. The research team intends to make the **smallest defensible revision** and then freeze the instruments before the main study.

## Suggested email / message to reviewers

> Dear Colleague,  
> We are preparing a small teaching-research study on a mobile interactive learning application for undergraduate Digital Signal Processing. We have developed two matched 12-item DSP concept tests for pre- and post-assessment and would appreciate a brief expert content review. The review focuses on relevance, clarity, distractor quality, and approximate equivalence of matched Form A/Form B items. The task normally takes about 20–30 minutes. No student data are involved. Your feedback will be used only to correct clear content or wording problems before a small pilot and the subsequent classroom study. Thank you for your help.

## Research-team handling

After receiving the reviews:

1. retain reviewer codes and ratings;
2. summarize the number and relevant expertise of reviewers;
3. record any item revision and its reason;
4. do not describe the instrument as broadly “validated” on the basis of this review alone;
5. proceed to the short AB/BA pilot only after any major content problem is resolved.
