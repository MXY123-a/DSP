## Completion rule

Use the course/study definition already in place for completion. Do not redefine completion after examining learning outcomes.

## Time field

For the experimental group, app duration is an elapsed-time proxy. For the control group, time may be self-reported or administratively recorded. Do not present the two measures as identical measurement methods.

## Deviation note

If `Delivered as planned?` is `No`, briefly record the actual event in the relevant deviation column and add enough detail in `Notes` to reconstruct what happened later.
